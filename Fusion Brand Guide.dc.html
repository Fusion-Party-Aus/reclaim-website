The Party
FUSION

What we are. A progressive, evidence-based political party. The entity, the values, the people. Built from the integration of science, technology, whistleblower advocacy, and progressive policy movements.

The Campaign System

What we intend to do. The campaign direction, visual language, and design system. Reclaim Our Economy. Reclaim Our Future. The aesthetic of urgency without the scapegoat.

05

Photography

Real photos of real places and real people — infrastructure, community events, campaign moments, public spaces. Every photo needs a purpose. The diagonal split treatment is the signature device. No AI imagery. No stock photography.

The Diagonal Split — Template
Step 1 — Treat the Left Side
Infrastructure: stark B&W desaturation. People: reduced saturation only — keep warmth and skin tones readable, don't go stark grey.
Step 2 — Diagonal Clip
A sharp diagonal running roughly top-centre to bottom-right divides the image. The clip is roughly 40% from the right at top, 60% at bottom.
Step 3 — Choose Duotone Colour
Infrastructure: Purple — shadows = Brand Purple (#5C006B), highlights = white. People & community: Teal — shadows = #006B5C, highlights = white. One colour per image; don't mix.
Step 4 — Rings Watermark
Place the white mono rings logo bottom-right of the duotone area at ~15% image height. Opacity ~85%.
Infrastructure — Purple Duotone
People & Community — Teal Duotone
People photos keep colour warmth on the left side — don't fully desaturate. The teal duotone on the right anchors them to the brand without washing out skin tones. Photos must have a clear purpose: community events, canvassing, candidate moments — not generic posed smiles.
