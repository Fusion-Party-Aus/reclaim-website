# Fusion branch website

Language for the state platforms and their supporting published material.

## Language

**Branch**:
A state organisation of Fusion, such as Victoria or Queensland, with its own platform and public identity. A branch here is distinct from a Git branch.

**Site**:
An independently identified public Fusion website with its own canonical hostname and publishing context. A Site may be National or be associated with a Branch; Site and Branch are not interchangeable.

**Publishing gateway**:
The required path for routine CMS writes, where content changes are checked against ownership, publication scope, and reference rules before they are applied.

**Break-glass administrator**:
A named technical maintainer permitted to bypass routine publishing checks only to recover service, with an audit trail and post-action review.

**Policy**:
A proposal in a branch's platform, with a stated direction and supporting rationale. A policy can be a current-term priority or a longer-term direction.

**Delivery horizon**:
Whether a policy is intended as a current-term priority or a longer-term direction. It describes timing rather than the strength of evidence or costing status.

**Research resource**:
A published dataset, methodology, submission, briefing, report, model or register that makes evidence and assumptions available for scrutiny.

**Electorate**:
An electoral district represented on the site with its candidate information. An archived electorate record belongs to a previous campaign rather than the current candidate list.
